# 📖 User Manual - Lunch Ordering System

Welcome to the Lunch Ordering System! This guide explains how to use the system for daily lunch ordering.

**Note**: This system is completely separate from the LISTO Medical App and operates independently.

## 🍽️ System Overview

The lunch ordering system automates the process of:
1. **Voting** for restaurants (morning)
2. **Collecting orders** from team members (late morning)
3. **Coordinating delivery** and payment (lunch time)
4. **Tracking** and notifications (automatic)

## 👥 User Roles

### 🙋 Regular Users (Team Members)
- Vote for restaurant preferences
- Place individual lunch orders  
- Receive notifications and updates
- View order status on dashboard

### 👑 Lunch Coordinator
- Manage daily sessions
- Contact restaurants and place group orders
- Handle payments and delivery coordination
- Generate reports and summaries

### 🔧 System Administrator  
- Set up and maintain the system
- Manage restaurant list
- Configure automation settings
- Troubleshoot technical issues

## 🎯 Daily Workflow

### Morning (9:00 AM - 11:00 AM)

#### 1. Restaurant Voting Opens
- **System automatically** starts voting for today's lunch
- **Email notifications** sent to team (optional)
- **Dashboard updated** to show voting is active

#### 2. Cast Your Vote
**Option A: Using the Dashboard**
1. Go to the lunch dashboard page in SharePoint
2. Click **"Vote for Restaurant"** button
3. Select your preferred restaurant
4. Add your name (optional for anonymous voting)
5. Click **"Cast Vote"**

**Option B: Using Microsoft Forms**
1. Click the voting form link (sent via email)
2. Choose your preferred restaurant
3. Optionally indicate cuisine preferences and dietary needs
4. Submit the form

**Voting Tips**:
- You can vote anonymously by leaving the name field blank
- Consider dietary restrictions of your team
- Check delivery times if you have meeting conflicts

#### 3. Voting Closes Automatically
- System determines winner based on vote count
- Tie votes are resolved by random selection
- Winner announcement sent to coordinator
- Dashboard updated with selected restaurant

### Late Morning (11:00 AM - 12:30 PM)

#### 4. Order Collection Opens
- **System automatically** opens order collection
- **Email notifications** sent with restaurant selection
- **Dashboard updated** to show order form

#### 5. Place Your Order  
**Using the Dashboard**:
1. Click **"Place Order"** button
2. Fill out your information:
   - **Name**: Required for order tracking
   - **Email**: For confirmations (optional)
   - **Restaurant**: Pre-filled with winner
   - **Menu Items**: List what you want to order
   - **Estimated Cost**: Help with payment planning
   - **Special Instructions**: Dietary needs, modifications
   - **Payment Method**: How you'll pay
3. Click **"Place Order"**

**Order Tips**:
- **Be specific**: Include sizes, options, sides
- **Include prices** if known (helps with group total)
- **Note allergies** or dietary restrictions clearly
- **Double-check** your order before submitting

**Example Order**:
```
Menu Items:
- Large Margherita Pizza - $14.99
- Caesar Salad (no croutons) - $8.99
- Diet Coke - $2.50

Special Instructions:
- Gluten-free pizza crust if available
- Extra cheese on pizza

Estimated Total: $26.48
```

#### 6. Order Confirmation
- **Automatic confirmation** email sent
- **Order appears** on dashboard
- **Coordinator notified** of new order

### Lunch Time (12:30 PM onwards)

#### 7. Order Collection Closes
- **System automatically** closes order collection
- **Final summary** sent to coordinator
- **Group order** ready for placement with restaurant

#### 8. Order Placement & Delivery
- **Coordinator contacts** restaurant with group order
- **Payment collection** begins (cash/digital)
- **Delivery tracking** and notifications
- **Food distribution** when delivered

## 📱 Dashboard Guide

### Status Cards
- **Voting Status**: Shows if voting is open/closed and deadline
- **Order Collection**: Shows if orders are open/closed
- **Selected Restaurant**: Shows winner and order count

### Action Buttons
- **Vote for Restaurant**: Opens voting form (during voting period)
- **Place Order**: Opens order form (during order collection)
- **Admin Panel**: Coordinator tools (restricted access)

### Current Orders Section  
- Shows all submitted orders for today
- Updates in real-time as orders come in
- Displays names, items, and costs

### Statistics
- **Participants**: Number of people who voted or ordered
- **Votes Cast**: Total votes in today's voting
- **Orders Placed**: Number of lunch orders submitted
- **Estimated Total**: Sum of all order costs

## 📧 Notifications

### Email Notifications (Optional)
- **Voting reminders**: When voting opens and before it closes
- **Winner announcements**: Restaurant selection results
- **Order confirmations**: When you successfully place an order
- **Deadline warnings**: Before order collection closes

### Dashboard Notifications
- **Real-time updates**: Status changes automatically
- **Toast messages**: Success/error messages for actions
- **Visual indicators**: Color-coded status information

## 💳 Payment Methods

### Cash Payment
- Pay the lunch coordinator directly
- Bring exact change or specify denomination needs
- Payment collected before or after delivery

### Digital Payment (Venmo/PayPal/etc.)
- Send payment to coordinator's account
- Include your name and "lunch [date]" in payment note
- Payment typically due when placing order

### Company Card
- Available if approved by management
- Requires coordinator to have company card access
- Usually for special occasions or team events

### Direct Payment
- Pay the restaurant directly (for pickup orders)
- Coordinate with team to avoid confusion
- Less common for delivery orders

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Can't Access Dashboard
- **Check SharePoint permissions**: You need at least read access
- **Try different browser**: Chrome, Edge, or Firefox
- **Clear browser cache**: Ctrl+F5 to hard refresh
- **Contact administrator**: If permissions are the issue

### Vote/Order Buttons Disabled
- **Check timing**: Buttons are only active during appropriate periods
- **Verify session status**: May be no active session today
- **Refresh page**: Status may be outdated

### Form Submission Errors
- **Check required fields**: All required information must be filled
- **Verify email format**: If providing email address
- **Try again**: Temporary connectivity issues may resolve
- **Contact coordinator**: If problems persist

### Not Receiving Notifications
- **Check spam folder**: Automated emails may be filtered
- **Verify email address**: Ensure it's correct in your profile
- **Check notification settings**: May be disabled
- **Ask coordinator**: To verify you're on the distribution list

## 📋 Best Practices

### For Voting
- **Vote early**: Don't wait until the last minute
- **Consider others**: Think about team dietary restrictions
- **Check menus**: Look at options before voting
- **Be flexible**: Your first choice may not win

### For Ordering
- **Order promptly**: Don't wait until deadline
- **Be specific**: Detailed orders prevent mistakes
- **Include costs**: Helps with payment coordination
- **Double-check**: Review before submitting

### For Payment
- **Pay on time**: When coordinator requests payment
- **Include details**: Name and date in payment notes
- **Keep receipts**: For digital payments
- **Communicate issues**: Tell coordinator about payment problems

### For Coordinators
- **Monitor dashboard**: Check order status regularly
- **Communicate clearly**: Keep team updated on timing
- **Have backup plan**: In case restaurant is unavailable
- **Document issues**: For system improvements
