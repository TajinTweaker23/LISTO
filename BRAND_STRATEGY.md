## 🛡️ **Privacy & Ethics Framework**

### **Core Principles**
1. **Anonymous by design**: No real names or photos required
2. **Local-first data**: Sensitive information stays on device
3. **Consent-driven sharing**: Users control what data contributes to insights
4. **Transparent AI**: Explainable recommendations and pattern detection

### **Data Handling**
- **Health data**: Encrypted, local storage with optional cloud backup
- **Social interactions**: Anonymous identifiers only
- **AI training**: Aggregated, anonymized patterns for community benefit
